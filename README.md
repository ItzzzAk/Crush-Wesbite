# 💖 Do You Love Me? - A Fun Playful Web App 💖

![App Screenshot](https://your-image-link-here.com/screenshot.png)

**Do You Love Me?** is a cute, fun, and playful web application designed to bring a smile to your face! 😍 Whether you're trying to make your crush laugh or just want to have some fun with your friends, this app is perfect for adding a touch of romance and humor to your day. 💌

The app starts with an adorable question: **Do you love me?** 🥰 But here's the twist—the mischievous **"No"** button keeps dodging your clicks, making it almost impossible to reject the love! 💃 Every time you try to click **No**, the button moves away, teasing you into finally hitting **Yes**. And when you do... get ready for a shower of hearts and a flirty surprise message! 😘🎉

You can even create your **own custom messages** and share them with your crush or friends. Watch as they try to say **No** but just can't resist the charm! 😉

Perfect for **Valentine's Day**, anniversaries, or just for some playful fun! 💝
